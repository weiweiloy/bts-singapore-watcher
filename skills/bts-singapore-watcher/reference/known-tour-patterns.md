# How Big Hit announce tour ticket sales

Typical signals that a "STAY TUNED" entry is about to become a live sale:

- The phrase "STAY TUNED" is replaced with a date and time for ticket on-sale.
- A link to a ticketing partner appears (commonly Weverse, Interpark, or a local partner — for Singapore likely a local promoter or Klook / Ticketmaster SG).
- A "Notice" or "News" item is added near the date.
- Pre-sale information for Weverse members appears before general sale.
- For the 2026 ARIRANG tour specifically, the venue name is currently hidden behind "STAY TUNED" — when a real venue name appears (e.g. "NATIONAL STADIUM" or "SINGAPORE INDOOR STADIUM"), that is itself a high-confidence signal.

If any of these appear in a new snapshot, treat it as a high-confidence ticket release signal.
